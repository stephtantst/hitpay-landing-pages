ONLINE · PRICED IN YOUR CURRENCY

Stop losing international carts to unfamiliar currency and unexpected FX charges

Online stores that price only in their home currency often lose international shoppers at checkout, when a customer can't tell what they're actually being charged. Adaptive Pricing brings the same mechanic as Borderless QR online: it detects a customer's location by IP address and automatically displays the converted amount in their local currency alongside relevant local payment methods, while the business still creates every payment request in its own home currency. The FX rate is locked when the checkout page loads and refreshes only if the customer reloads it, so the price never shifts mid-payment. Because it applies at the HitPay hosted checkout itself, Adaptive Pricing works inside an existing online store integration — Shopify, Wix, WooCommerce, and more — with no rebuild required.

  • ✓ Merchant prices stay in one home currency — no separate link per market
  • ✓ Currency selector shown only to customers whose local currency differs from the merchant's
  • ✓ Turned on in Checkout Customisation settings — no code required
  • ✓ Works with your existing online store integration — Shopify, Wix, WooCommerce, and more — with no extra setup per platform
  • ✓ Not available on the default payment link, open-amount links, or recurring billing

Frequently asked questions: Cross-border payments

Everything Singapore, Malaysia, and Philippine businesses need to know about accepting payments from customers across Asia-Pacific.

What is HitPay and how does it work for businesses accepting cross-border payments?

HitPay is a Singapore-headquartered payment platform, licensed as a Major Payment Institution (PS20200643) under the Monetary Authority of Singapore, that lets Singapore, Malaysia, and Philippine businesses accept cross-border payments from customers across Asia-Pacific. Businesses choose from four products — Borderless QR, Multi-Currency Products, Adaptive Pricing, and Multi-Currency Payment Links — depending on whether they sell online or in person and who sets the exchange rate. There are zero monthly fees; standard transaction fees vary by payment method.

What cross-border payment methods does HitPay support, and in which markets are they popular?

HitPay supports 14 cross-border payment methods across nine Asia-Pacific markets: PayNow (Singapore), UPI (India), WeChat Pay (China), Touch 'n Go and DuitNow (Malaysia), QRPH (Philippines), VietQR and ZaloPay (Vietnam), PromptPay, TrueMoney, and LINE Pay (Thailand), QRIS (Indonesia), and PayCo, KakaoPay, and NaverPay (South Korea). Most support both online checkout and in-person QR acceptance; PayCo, KakaoPay, NaverPay, and Touch 'n Go are online-only today.

What is the difference between Borderless QR and Multi-Currency Products?

Both are in-person, point-of-sale products, but they serve opposite scenarios. Borderless QR suits a fixed storefront serving foreign tourists — the business charges in its home currency and HitPay converts automatically so customers pay in their own local currency. Multi-Currency Products suits a business travelling to sell overseas — the merchant prices the product directly in the foreign currency, with no automatic HitPay conversion.

What is the difference between Adaptive Pricing and Multi-Currency Payment Links?

Both are online products, but pricing control differs. Adaptive Pricing keeps prices in the merchant's home currency and automatically shows international customers the converted amount in their local currency at checkout, with HitPay handling the FX. Multi-Currency Payment Links let a merchant create a link denominated directly in the customer's currency, with the merchant — not HitPay — setting that price.

Which cross-border product should an online business use?

Online businesses that price everything in their home currency and want HitPay to handle currency conversion automatically should use Adaptive Pricing — it detects the customer's location and displays local pricing with no extra setup per currency. Businesses that prefer to set a specific price directly in a customer's currency, such as when quoting a fixed rate to an overseas client, should use Multi-Currency Payment Links instead.

Which cross-border product should an in-person or overseas pop-up business use?

A fixed storefront serving foreign tourists — a café, retail shop, or restaurant — should use Borderless QR, which lets customers scan and pay in their home currency while the business receives its own home currency. A business travelling overseas for a market, pop-up, or event should use Multi-Currency Products, pricing items directly in that country's local currency.

What fees apply to cross-border payments?

An FX markup applies on top of the mid-market rate when HitPay handles the currency conversion — on Borderless QR and Adaptive Pricing. Multi-Currency Products and Multi-Currency Payment Links carry no automatic FX conversion, since the merchant sets the price directly in the foreign currency. Standard payment processing fees also apply, and customers are never charged an additional FX fee. Full rates are at hitpayapp.com/pricing.

How is the cross-border exchange rate determined?

HitPay uses the mid-market exchange rate plus a standard FX markup — see hitpayapp.com/pricing for exact rates. The rate refreshes daily at 6:00am SGT and is cached for the rest of the day, so every transaction, settlement, and refund processed that day uses the same rate. For Adaptive Pricing specifically, the rate is locked when the customer's checkout page loads and only refreshes if the page is reloaded.

When do businesses receive their cross-border payout?

Cross-border payments are credited to a business's HitPay Balance on a next business day basis in Singapore, Malaysia, and the Philippines. The collected amount is automatically converted to the merchant's home currency and included in the regular daily payout, with no separate cross-border settlement cycle to track.

How do refunds work for cross-border payments?

A cross-border refund is issued in the original charge currency. The amount deducted from the merchant's home-currency wallet is converted at the live exchange rate at the time of the refund, not the rate used for the original transaction — so the merchant may see a small FX gain or loss depending on how rates moved between sale and refund.

How do businesses enable cross-border payment methods?

Businesses go to Settings > Payment Methods in their HitPay dashboard, click Connect on the desired payment method, and complete the required information. Partner providers typically review new requests within three to five business days. Once approved, businesses go to Integrations, select their sales channel, and enable the payment method for that channel.

Do businesses need additional hardware for in-person cross-border payments?

No. Borderless QR and Multi-Currency Products both work with the HitPay POS app a business already uses — no additional terminal, reader, or hardware purchase is required. A business only needs a smartphone or tablet running the existing HitPay POS app to accept a tourist's local QR payment method.

Does Adaptive Pricing work with recurring billing or subscriptions?

No. Adaptive Pricing currently supports one-time hosted checkout payments only. Recurring billing and subscription charges fall outside its scope, so a business billing customers on a subscription basis should quote prices in a single settlement currency rather than relying on Adaptive Pricing's automatic local-currency detection.

What happens if a customer's currency is not supported by Adaptive Pricing?

If the customer's detected country maps to a currency Adaptive Pricing doesn't support, or one that matches the merchant's own default currency, the checkout simply displays the merchant's default currency and payment methods, with no currency selector shown. The customer completes the payment exactly as a domestic customer would.

How long does it take to set up HitPay cross-border payments for a Singapore, Malaysian, or Philippine business?

Businesses that already hold a HitPay account can enable cross-border payment methods and Adaptive Pricing directly from their dashboard, with partner review typically taking three to five business days per payment method. New businesses first complete HitPay account verification — approval typically takes one to three business days — before connecting cross-border methods.

What support does HitPay provide to businesses using cross-border payments?

HitPay provides live chat and email support to help businesses connect cross-border payment methods, troubleshoot checkout issues, and reconcile cross-border settlements, alongside a self-serve help centre covering setup, fees, and refund handling for every supported market.

How do businesses test Adaptive Pricing before going live?

Businesses simulate a specific country by appending a "+location_XX" suffix to a test customer's email, where XX is a two-letter ISO country code — for example, test+location_FR@example.com for France. Entering this in the Payment Request API's email field, or in the dashboard's send-by-email flow, makes the checkout present currency and pricing exactly as a customer from that country would see it.

Can businesses build a custom cross-border checkout using HitPay's API?

Yes. Developers can integrate cross-border payments through the Payment Request API, which supports the localised_pricing parameter for Adaptive Pricing on hosted checkout pages, or through the Borderless QR API for custom in-person integrations. HitPay's Developer Cross-Border Guide compares both approaches for teams building their own checkout flow.

Are Singapore, Malaysia, and Philippine businesses all eligible for every cross-border product?

Eligibility depends on the specific payment method and product rather than a blanket rule — most of HitPay's 14 cross-border payment methods are available to merchants in Singapore, Malaysia, and the Philippines, though partner coverage can vary by market. Businesses should check availability for their specific payment method in Settings > Payment Methods after signing up.

How much does HitPay cost for businesses accepting cross-border payments?

There is zero monthly fee for accepting cross-border payments in any market. Businesses pay a standard per-transaction processing fee plus, where HitPay handles the FX conversion (Borderless QR and Adaptive Pricing), an FX markup over the mid-market rate. Multi-Currency Products and Multi-Currency Payment Links carry no FX markup, since the merchant sets the price directly. Full pricing is at hitpayapp.com/pricing.

Does HitPay offer a way for developers to build a custom cross-border payment integration?

Yes. Developers can use the Borderless QR API for API-level access to the same FX and QR-network mechanic used in HitPay's native in-person Borderless QR product, or the Payment Request API for the adaptive, currency-aware pricing mechanic behind Adaptive Pricing — both let a team build their own POS or checkout experience instead of using HitPay's native interface.

What is the difference between using HitPay's native Borderless QR product and the Borderless QR API?

HitPay's native Borderless QR product runs entirely inside the existing HitPay POS app, using HitPay's own exchange rate and interface. The Borderless QR API exposes the same underlying FX and QR-network mechanic so a developer or partner can build the acceptance flow into their own custom POS or checkout, rather than using HitPay's native app.

HOW IT WORKS

One HitPay account, four ways to get paid across borders

HitPay is a payment platform that lets Singapore, Malaysia, and Philippine businesses accept cross-border payments from customers across Asia-Pacific, choosing between four products depending on whether they sell online or in person, and who sets the exchange rate. A café near Orchard Road in Singapore can let a tourist scan and pay in Chinese yuan via WeChat Pay; a boutique at Pavilion Kuala Lumpur in Malaysia can let a Thai shopper check out in Thai baht via PromptPay; and an online seller shipping from Makati in the Philippines can let a Vietnamese buyer pay in Vietnamese dong via VietQR — all without opening a foreign bank account or holding foreign currency.

Businesses that let HitPay handle the conversion — Borderless QR in person, Adaptive Pricing online — pay an FX markup over the mid-market rate and receive their own home currency automatically. Businesses that prefer to set the foreign-currency price themselves use Multi-Currency Products in person or Multi-Currency Payment Links online, with no automatic FX markup. Every checkout, in every product, runs on HitPay's PCI DSS Level 1 certified infrastructure, so card details from cross-border customers are protected to the same standard as domestic payments. Developers building a custom POS or checkout can access the same mechanics directly via the Borderless QR API and Payment Request API — see the developer section above.
