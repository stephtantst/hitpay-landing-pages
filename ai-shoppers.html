Frequently asked questions

What HitPay Online Store merchants in Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines most often ask about AI shopping.

What is HitPay Online Store and how does it support AI shopping for businesses?

HitPay Online Store is a payment platform for merchants in Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines. It accepts 50+ payment methods, charges zero monthly fees, and settles payments next business day. Since 2026, every HitPay Online Store is automatically set up for AI shopping — ChatGPT and other AI tools can discover products, recommend them, and complete purchases on behalf of customers at no extra cost.

Does a HitPay Online Store merchant need to do anything to enable AI shopping?

No. Every HitPay Online Store already has AI shopping features active — the product catalog is visible to AI tools, the store accepts AI-initiated checkouts, and the AI-readiness signal is in place. There is nothing to click, configure, or pay for. If a merchant's store is live on HitPay, it is already set up for AI shoppers.

Which AI tools can find and shop from a HitPay Online Store?

Any AI assistant that can shop online can work with HitPay Online Store — including tools built on ChatGPT and Perplexity. As new AI shopping tools launch from OpenAI, Google, Anthropic and others, HitPay Online Store merchants will be ready for them automatically, without any action required.

How does ChatGPT find and recommend products from a HitPay Online Store?

HitPay creates a structured product catalog that AI tools can read — including names, prices, and stock status. This updates every hour. When a shopper asks ChatGPT to recommend something a HitPay merchant sells, ChatGPT can read the catalog and surface the product. Merchants do not need to do anything — the catalog feed is generated automatically.

Can an AI assistant complete a full checkout at a HitPay Online Store?

Yes. HitPay Online Store supports agentic checkout — where an AI assistant completes the entire purchase on behalf of a customer. The customer tells their AI what they want to buy. The AI finds the product, fills in delivery details, and creates the order. The customer pays. The merchant sees the order in their HitPay dashboard exactly like any other order.

Will a HitPay merchant know if an order came from an AI assistant?

Orders placed by AI assistants appear in the HitPay dashboard exactly like any other order. Merchants fulfil them the same way — pack, ship, or prepare for pickup as normal. No special handling is required.

Is customer data shared with AI tools when they shop at a HitPay Online Store?

No. Only publicly listed product information — names, prices, and stock status — is visible to AI tools. Customer data, order history, and payment details are never shared. HitPay is licensed by MAS (PS20200643) and is PCI DSS Level 1 compliant.

How much does HitPay Online Store cost for merchants in Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines?

HitPay Online Store has zero monthly fees and zero setup fees across Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines. Merchants pay a per-transaction rate only when a customer pays them — including AI-initiated orders. All AI shopping features are included at no extra cost. See hitpayapp.com/pricing for current transaction rates.

What support does HitPay provide to Online Store merchants in Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines?

HitPay provides live chat support, email support, and a self-serve Help Centre at support.hitpayapp.com. Merchants in Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines can access onboarding guides, payment integration documentation, and account management support. Enterprise merchants can speak with a dedicated account manager.

Does the HitPay AI shopping feature apply to payment link users or in-person merchants?

Not yet — AI shopping features are specific to merchants with a HitPay Online Store. Payment link users and in-person merchants using POS or QR codes are not yet covered. Merchants who want access can set up a free HitPay Online Store at hitpayapp.com.

How do businesses in Singapore sign up for a HitPay Online Store?

Singapore merchants register with their ACRA business registration number or personal NRIC. There are no setup fees and no monthly fees. Approval takes 1–3 business days. Once approved, merchants create their online store, list products, and the store is automatically set up for AI shopping with no extra steps.

How do businesses in Malaysia sign up for a HitPay Online Store?

Malaysian merchants register with their SSM business registration number and MyKad. Sole proprietors can apply with their personal MyKad. Approval takes 1–3 business days at no cost. Once live, the Online Store is automatically visible to AI shopping tools — no extra configuration needed.

How do businesses in the Philippines sign up for a HitPay Online Store?

Philippine merchants register with a valid SEC or DTI registration certificate plus a government-issued ID. Sole proprietors use their DTI registration. Approval takes 1–3 business days at no cost. Once live, the store is automatically set up for AI shopping — including ChatGPT product discovery and agentic checkout.

What happens if a product is out of stock when an AI tries to purchase it?

HitPay's product catalog feed updates every hour with current stock status. If a product is marked as out of stock in the HitPay dashboard, AI tools will see it as unavailable and will not attempt to purchase it. Merchants manage stock the same way they always have — through the HitPay dashboard.

How quickly does HitPay update product information for AI shopping tools?

HitPay's AI product catalog updates automatically every hour. When a merchant adds a new product, changes a price, or marks an item as out of stock, the updated information is available to AI shopping tools within one hour. No manual publishing step is required.
