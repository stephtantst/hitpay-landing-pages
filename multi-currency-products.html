Frequently asked questions: Multi-Currency Products

Everything merchants need to know before pricing a catalogue in multiple currencies.

What is Multi-Currency Products?

Multi-Currency Products is a HitPay Point of Sale feature that lets a merchant price the same product in multiple currencies at once — for example SGD 3, MYR 9, and PHP 140 on a single item. Each currency gets its own manually set price, so overseas customers pay a fixed local-currency amount using their own local payment methods, while the merchant is always paid out in their home currency.

Which HitPay channel supports Multi-Currency Products?

Multi-Currency Products is currently available for HitPay Point of Sale only, on both the Web Dashboard and the HitPay mobile app. It is not supported on the Online Store. Online merchants who want overseas customers to see localised pricing at checkout should use Adaptive Pricing instead.

How does a merchant set a price for a specific currency?

From the Web Dashboard or mobile app, a merchant opens a product under Point of Sale > Products, selects Add a price by currency under the Pricing section, chooses the currency, and enters the fixed amount. This can be repeated for every currency the merchant wants to support, and saved per product or per product variant.

What happens if a merchant hasn't set a price for a currency?

HitPay resolves the displayed price in order: first any manual price the merchant has set for that currency, then the exchange rate configured in the merchant's store settings, and finally the live mid-market exchange rate, which refreshes every 6 hours. The customer always sees a price — the merchant is never forced to set every currency in advance.

Do multi-currency prices update automatically when exchange rates change?

No. A manually set currency price stays fixed until the merchant changes it — it does not track live exchange rate movements. Only currencies with no manual price set fall back to automatic real-time conversion, refreshed every 6 hours, the same as standard Borderless QR behaviour.

Can a merchant bulk import multi-currency prices?

Not yet. Multi-currency prices must be set individually on each product or product variant through the Web Dashboard or the HitPay mobile app — there is currently no bulk import option for currency-specific pricing.

Does Multi-Currency Products work for products with variants?

Yes. Each variant of a product — such as a size or colour — can carry its own independent set of currency prices, set the same way as a standalone product under that variant's Pricing section.

Which currencies can a merchant add prices for?

A merchant can add a price for any currency supported by HitPay Borderless QR in their market, covering local payment methods such as PayNow, DuitNow, Touch 'n Go, QR Ph, and QRIS. The full list of supported countries and payment methods is available in HitPay's Borderless QR documentation.

How does a customer end up paying in their own currency at checkout?

The merchant sets the checkout currency to match the customer's currency — under Checkout Currency in the mobile app, or the checkout currency selector in Web POS — before ringing up the sale. Once set, every product price on that device displays and charges in the selected currency for the rest of that transaction.

What is the difference between Multi-Currency Products and Borderless QR or Adaptive Pricing?

Borderless QR and Adaptive Pricing let HitPay handle the currency conversion automatically, at a rate the merchant doesn't set. Multi-Currency Products is the opposite: the merchant fixes the exact price in each foreign currency ahead of time, which suits a business travelling overseas to sell at a pop-up, market, or trade fair, where the merchant wants full control over the local price rather than a converted one.

How much does Multi-Currency Products cost?

There is zero monthly fee to use Multi-Currency Products. Standard HitPay payment processing fees apply per transaction as normal, and because the merchant sets the price directly, there is no additional FX markup on top. Full rates are at hitpayapp.com/pricing.

How long does it take to set up Multi-Currency Products?

Setting prices per currency on a product takes minutes in the Web Dashboard or mobile app. Connecting the underlying local payment methods a merchant wants to accept, such as DuitNow or QRIS, goes through partner review, which typically takes 3–5 business days before those methods go live.

Does Multi-Currency Products require managing a separate product catalogue per currency?

No. A merchant manages a single product list, with every currency price attached to the same product or variant record. There is no duplicate catalogue to keep in sync per market, and stock counts and sales reports reconcile against that one product list regardless of how many currencies a sale was priced in.

What support does HitPay provide for Multi-Currency Products?

HitPay provides live chat support, email support, and a self-serve help centre at support.hitpayapp.com covering Multi-Currency Products setup, currency pricing, and payout reconciliation. Merchants in Singapore, Malaysia, and the Philippines can reach support in English.
