Frequently asked questions

Can international vendors from Thailand, Malaysia, or Indonesia use HitPay at the fair? +
Yes. International vendors from Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Japan, and the Philippines can accept payments via Borderless PayNow QR and Visa/Mastercard. No Singapore bank account or Singapore entity is required. Payouts are made to a home-country account in the local currency.
Do I need to buy or rent a card terminal to accept payments? +
No. HitPay's Tap to Pay feature turns any iPhone (iOS 16.4+) or Android into a payment terminal. Card terminals are optional. International vendors may also use the Borderless PayNow QR — no hardware required at all.
How long does HitPay account approval take? +
Account approval typically takes 1–3 business days. It is recommended to sign up at least one week before the fair opens to allow time for setup and testing.
When will I receive my payout after the fair? +
Singapore vendors typically receive payouts within 1–3 banking days. International vendors receive payouts in their home currency, with timing varying by country. Payouts are processed automatically — no manual action required after the sale.
Are there any monthly fees or minimum commitments? +
No. HitPay charges SGD 0 in monthly fees. There is no contract, no setup fee, and no minimum commitment. Vendors pay only a small percentage per transaction when a sale is made.
